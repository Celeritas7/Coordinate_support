Stand-ins for real parts until clearance to the assembly is read. The follower keeps Gap to boxes from every box, as well as Min gap from the fixed pipe; where both cannot hold, the report says so. Boxes show in 3D on the offset run.

Only the spots closer than Min gap move, measured surface to surface. Both ends and end directions stay; no straight gets shorter than Min straight (or than it already is). Corners move as little as the gap allows. Other parts are not checked yet.
