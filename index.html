Fewest points: after routing, drops every point the curve doesn't need. The radius still holds and the curve moves at most Max shift. Target points are always kept.

Keep clear of CAD parts (IGES / STEP solids)

Flat and cylindrical faces are read with their real outline and holes, so the gap to them is exact (a hose through a hole or past a plate edge sees that edge); other faces count as a box. Parts at a station, and small parts (80 mm or less) the station line runs through, are taken as clips and clamps and left unticked: the hose may touch them. Tick or untick any part. A 23 MB IGES takes about 10 s to read.

Lines + arcs: the .pts file holds only the corner points of a polyline. In Creo, build the curve with Curve through points, Single radius, and type the radius from the file header. The corners are moved until every arc and straight passes through your points; the radius is pushed from Min radius up toward Min radius × Safety factor.

Check limits

Kink: turn at one point. Spacing ratio: longer gap over shorter gap either side of a point. Moved: flag points further than this from the earlier version.

Points are checked exactly as loaded; nothing is re-routed or smoothed. Opening or dropping files replaces the list. Earlier versions match by cable name. STEP and IGES files become one cable per datum name prefix (C1_01, C1_02 → C1).

Pipe? auto lets the app decide; pipe takes the part even when it looks too short or thin; not a pipe skips it. Saved for this file name, here and in the Inspector. Hover a row for faces, placement and CSYS.

The pipe is re-routed so a straight runs along each ticked bore, covering it plus Margin at both sides. Both ends and end directions stay; existing corners move as little as possible, a corner is added only when needed. Min straight applies between bends. Other corners are kept: parts the pipe bends around are not read yet.
